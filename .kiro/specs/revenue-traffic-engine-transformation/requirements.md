# Requirements Document

## Introduction

This document outlines the comprehensive transformation of BlogCraft AI from a basic "ChatGPT wrapper" content generator into a sophisticated "Revenue/Traffic Engine" platform. The transformation aims to achieve ₹20-30L/month revenue by implementing outcome-based AI, full automation loops, personal AI brain capabilities, and advanced monetization features that guarantee measurable business results for users.

## Glossary

- **Revenue_Engine**: The core system that transforms content creation into measurable business outcomes through automation and optimization
- **Traffic_Engine**: The subsystem responsible for driving organic traffic growth through SEO, viral prediction, and multi-platform optimization
- **Personal_AI_Brain**: An adaptive AI system that learns user preferences, success patterns, and continuously improves content performance
- **Outcome_Based_AI**: AI systems that optimize for specific business metrics (traffic, engagement, conversions) rather than just content quality
- **Automation_Loop**: End-to-end automated workflows that require minimal human intervention (research → generate → design → schedule → analyze → optimize)
- **Viral_Prediction_Engine**: AI system that scores content for viral potential and provides optimization recommendations
- **Monetization_Engine**: System for automatic affiliate insertion, CTA generation, lead capture, and funnel creation
- **Multi_Platform_Domination**: Automated content distribution and optimization across all major platforms with performance tracking
- **Content_DNA_Analyzer**: System that reverse-engineers successful content to identify patterns and replicate success
- **Revenue_Attribution_System**: Analytics system that ties content performance directly to business outcomes and revenue generation

## Requirements

### Requirement 1: Outcome-Based AI System

**User Story:** As a business owner, I want AI that guarantees measurable results (traffic growth, engagement, revenue), so that I can achieve predictable business outcomes rather than just content creation.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Outcome_Based_AI SHALL generate content optimized for specific business metrics (traffic, engagement, conversions, revenue)
2. WHEN content is generated, THE Viral_Prediction_Engine SHALL provide a virality score (1-100) with 85%+ accuracy
3. THE Traffic_Engine SHALL guarantee 3x traffic growth within 90 days or provide optimization recommendations
4. WHEN content underperforms, THE Personal_AI_Brain SHALL automatically adjust strategy and regenerate improved versions
5. THE Revenue_Attribution_System SHALL track and report direct revenue impact from each piece of content
6. THE Content_DNA_Analyzer SHALL reverse-engineer top-performing content to identify success patterns
7. WHEN generating content, THE Outcome_Based_AI SHALL optimize for platform-specific engagement algorithms

### Requirement 2: Full Automation Loops

**User Story:** As a content creator, I want complete automation that works without human intervention, so that I can scale my content business while focusing on strategy.

#### Acceptance Criteria

2. WHEN a topic is provided, THE Revenue_Engine SHALL generate 30 days of multi-platform content within 10 minutes

4. WHEN content is published, THE Traffic_Engine SHALL automatically track performance and adjust future content strategy
